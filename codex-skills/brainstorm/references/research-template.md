# Research Template

Use this template for `docs/research/yyyymmdd-<task-name>.md`. Keep it concise and omit empty sections.

```markdown
# [Research Title]

## Goal

- what is being explored
- why it matters

## Context

- relevant files, components, constraints, and existing behavior

## Findings

- verified facts
- meaningful alternatives and trade-offs

## Decisions

- decisions made and their rationale

## Open Questions

- unresolved questions or risks

## Recommendation

- preferred direction and why
```
